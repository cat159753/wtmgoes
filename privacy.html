Privacy Policy — wtmgoes-uploader

Last updated: 2026-10-10

wtmgoes-uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Where the Money Goes" YouTube channel to upload the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

With the channel owner's permission, the tool uses YouTube API scopes to upload videos and captions and to read the channel's own name and video status. It does not access any other user's data.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
